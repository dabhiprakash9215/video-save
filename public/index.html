How to Fix "YouTube Bot Verification" on Vercel

YouTube restricts automated downloads on cloud server datacenter IPs (Vercel, AWS, GCP). You can solve this in 30 seconds using Cookies.

1

Method 1: Direct Cookie Settings (Fastest)

1. Install Chrome/Edge extension: Get cookies.txt LOCALLY.
2. Open YouTube while logged in and click the extension to copy cookies.
3. Click "Cookie Settings" at top-right of this page, paste the text, and click Save.

2

Method 2: Vercel Environment Variable

1. Go to your Vercel Dashboard > Project > Settings > Environment Variables.
2. Add variable key: YOUTUBE_COOKIES.
3. Paste your full Netscape cookies text into the Value and Redeploy.
